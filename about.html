Our beginning

Small beginnings, made with care.

Zaytro is a young masala brand founded by Garima Singh and Vishal Singh in Chiurapur, Babatpur, Varanasi. The idea is straightforward: create a thoughtful range of familiar spices and masalas for everyday home cooking, and build trust by being clear about what is in each pack.

There is no embellished tale of generations-old recipes or overnight success here. Zaytro is a business in its early days, growing its range one product at a time. We want the flavour, the care behind each product and the conversations with our customers to shape what comes next.

As Zaytro grows, we will share more about the people, ingredients and processes behind the products—only when the details are confirmed and ready to tell properly.
