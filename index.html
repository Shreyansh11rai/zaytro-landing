A Varanasi spice story, just getting started.

Zaytro is a young masala brand founded by Garima Singh and Vishal Singh. We are building a range for home cooks who want familiar ingredients, thoughtful blends and flavour that lets the meal speak for itself.

We are starting from Chiurapur, Babatpur, and growing one product at a time. The story is still being written—with care, honesty and a close connection to the people who cook with us.
